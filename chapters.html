Chapter-wise Quick Revision

Focus on one chapter at a time.

Chemical Reactions & Equations

  • Law of conservation of mass applies to balanced equations.
  • Types: combination, decomposition, displacement, double displacement.
  • Oxidation + reduction together is called redox.

Acids, Bases & Salts

  • pH < 7 acidic, pH 7 neutral, pH > 7 basic.
  • Indicators: litmus, phenolphthalein, methyl orange.
  • Important salts: baking soda, washing soda, bleaching powder.

Life Processes

  • Nutrition, respiration, transport, and excretion are core processes.
  • Heart has 4 chambers and supports double circulation.
  • Xylem transports water; phloem transports food.

Light: Reflection & Refraction

  • Mirror formula: 1/f = 1/v + 1/u
  • Lens formula: 1/f = 1/v - 1/u
  • Power of lens P = 1/f (diopter)

Electricity

  • Ohm's law: V = IR
  • Power: P = VI, P = I²R, P = V²/R
  • Series: same current, parallel: same potential difference.

Our Environment

  • Energy transfer in food chain follows the 10% law.
  • Biodegradable waste decomposes naturally.
  • Food webs are interlinked food chains.
